# Sales outcomes

This use case measures improvements in simulated outbound insurance and lending campaigns.

- Track pickup and engagement as separate KPIs, with leads concluded per lead uploaded as the business outcome.
- Reduce time spent on degraded caller IDs through policy-permitted rotation and controlled exploration.
- Promote welcome-message and voice variants only after the primary KPI wins and the guardrail is supported.
- Present per-workflow results against a no-change benchmark, including harmful changes and uncertainty.
- Prompt changes require safety validation and governance approval.
